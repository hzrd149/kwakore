<objective>
Implement phase 2 of the approved napplet-social design (`/home/robert/.claude/plans/robust-painting-trinket.md`, "Phases" item 2 plus its "Publishing" section, the error table and the phase-2 "Publish" tests). With the service signer, a socket client can vote on a napplet, react to it with an emoji, comment on it or reply to a comment, and delete its own reaction or comment.
